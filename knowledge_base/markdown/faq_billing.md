# Nimbus Cloud FAQ — Billing & Subscriptions

**Document ID:** KB-FAQ-002
**Version:** 2.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-15
**Category:** Billing

---

**Q1. What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) for Google
Workspace, JumpCloud, and Zoom subscriptions. Oracle NetSuite and
Professional Services engagements are typically invoiced via bank transfer
per the signed order form.

**Q2. When am I charged for my subscription?**
Google Workspace Flexible Plan and JumpCloud monthly plans are charged on
the same calendar day each month as your original purchase date. Annual
plans (Google Workspace Commitment, JumpCloud, Zoom, NetSuite) are billed
once per year on the contract/anniversary date.

**Q3. How do I get a copy of my invoice?**
Invoices are auto-emailed to your billing contact and available under
Account Settings → Billing → Invoice History. NetSuite contract invoices are
issued separately by our Finance team per your order form schedule.

**Q4. Can I switch between Google Workspace plans mid-cycle?**
Yes. Moving from Flexible to a Commitment (annual) plan takes effect
immediately. Downgrading from Commitment to Flexible only takes effect at
the end of the current 12-month term, since Commitment plans are a fixed
term per Google's reseller terms.

**Q5. Do you charge GST/VAT?**
Yes, applicable local tax is added based on the billing entity's country —
Singapore GST, Malaysia SST, or Indonesia PPN. Tax-exempt organizations
should contact billing@nimbus-cloud.example with documentation.

**Q6. My card payment failed — what happens to my account?**
For Google Workspace, JumpCloud, or Zoom, we retry failed payments
automatically 3 times over 7 days. Access is suspended after the third
failed attempt until payment is resolved. NetSuite and Professional Services
invoices follow separate payment terms per your contract (typically Net 30).

**Q7. How do I cancel my subscription?**
Go to Account Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription for Google Workspace,
JumpCloud, or Zoom. **Oracle NetSuite contracts cannot be self-cancelled** —
they are annual/multi-year commitments; any cancellation request must be
routed to a human Billing Specialist per the Refund Policy (KB-POLICY-001),
Section 6.

**Q8. I was charged twice this month — can I get a refund?**
Yes, duplicate charges are always refunded in full once verified, regardless
of product. See Refund Policy (KB-POLICY-001), Section 5 — Billing Errors.
Please include your invoice number when reporting this.

**Q9. Am I eligible for a refund if I just don't want the service anymore?**
This depends entirely on which product you're subscribed to — refund
eligibility differs significantly between, for example, Google Workspace and
Oracle NetSuite. Please refer to the Refund Policy (KB-POLICY-001), Section
2, for the specific rule for your product. If your product or situation
isn't clearly listed there, the case will be escalated for individual review.

**Q10. Who do I contact for Oracle NetSuite contract or Professional
Services billing questions?**
These are handled by your assigned Account Manager or Project Lead, not
general billing support. Please reference your contract/SOW number in any
inquiry so it can be routed correctly.

**Q11. What currency am I billed in?**
Billing currency is set at signup based on your region (SGD, MYR, or IDR)
and cannot be changed mid-subscription. Contact your Account Manager to
switch currency at renewal.

**Q12. Can I get a receipt for expense reporting before the invoice date?**
Yes, a provisional receipt can be generated on request via
billing@nimbus-cloud.example.
