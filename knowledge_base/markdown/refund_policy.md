## 1. Scope

This policy governs refunds for subscriptions and services billed directly
by Nimbus Cloud (Pte. Ltd. / Consulting Sdn Bhd / PT Nimbus Cloud), across the
following categories:

- Cloud subscriptions resold by Nimbus Cloud: **Google Workspace**, **Google
  Workspace for Education**, **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** usage,
  **Oracle NetSuite**, **JumpCloud**, **Zoom**, **Cisco Meraki**.
- **Professional Services**: implementation, migration, consulting,
  managed services delivered by Nimbus Cloud's own team.

It does not cover charges billed directly by the vendor to the customer
outside of a Nimbus Cloud reseller agreement — those follow the vendor's own
terms (e.g. Google's direct billing terms, Oracle's direct NetSuite MSA).

## 2. Refund Terms by Product Category

Refund eligibility **differs by vendor**, consistent with how each platform
is actually licensed. Always check the category before answering.

| Category | Billing Model | Refund Eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| Google Workspace — Flexible Plan | Monthly, cancel anytime | No refund for the current partial month; cancellation stops future billing only |
| Google Workspace — Annual/Commitment Plan | Annual, prepaid or monthly with 12-month lock-in | Full refund within **14 calendar days** of initial purchase; after that, the commitment continues to term-end with no refund, per standard Google reseller terms |
| Google Cloud Platform (GCP) usage | Pay-as-you-go, billed monthly on consumption | Consumption charges are **non-refundable** once resources are provisioned/used; billing errors follow Section 5 |
| Oracle NetSuite | Annual or multi-year contract, signed order form | **Non-cancelable and non-refundable** once the contract is signed and provisioning begins — standard NetSuite SaaS contract terms. Mid-term cancellation requests are always escalated (Section 6) |
| JumpCloud | Monthly or annual | Monthly: no refund, cancels future billing only. Annual: full refund within **14 calendar days** of initial purchase |
| Zoom licenses | Monthly or annual | Same pattern as Google Workspace above |
| Cisco Meraki — hardware | One-time purchase | **14-day return window** for unopened/unused hardware; opened/activated hardware is non-refundable |
| Cisco Meraki — license subscription | Annual, tied to hardware | Non-refundable once activated, as license activation is tied to the physical device |
| Professional Services (implementation, consulting, migration) | Fixed-bid or time & materials, milestone-based | **Non-refundable** once work has commenced or a milestone has been delivered and accepted by the customer |

## 3. Money-Back Guarantee (First-Time Subscribers Only)

- Where Section 2 specifies a 14-day window (Google Workspace Annual,
  JumpCloud Annual), this applies **once per customer/organization** for a
  **first-time** subscription to that specific product. A renewal or a
  second subscription is not eligible.
- Eligible refunds are processed to the original payment method within
  **5–10 business days**.

## 4. Non-Refundable Items (All Categories)

The following are **never** eligible for refund, regardless of product or
timing:

- Consumption-based charges already incurred (GCP usage, NetSuite overage
  fees, API usage).
- Professional services hours already delivered and accepted.
- Any subscription cancelled or refunded due to a confirmed **Terms of
  Service violation**, fraud, or a payment dispute/chargeback initiated by
  the customer's bank.
- NetSuite subscriptions past their contract signing date (Section 2).

## 5. Billing Errors

If a customer reports a duplicate charge, an incorrect amount, or a charge
after confirmed cancellation, and this is verified by the Billing team, a
**full refund of the erroneous amount** is issued within 5–10 business days.
This is not subject to the eligibility windows in Section 2.

## 6. Cases Requiring Human Review (Do Not Auto-Approve)

A support agent (human or automated) must **not** approve or promise a
refund outcome, and must escalate to a human Billing Specialist, when:

- The request involves an **Oracle NetSuite contract** (Section 2 — always
  requires Finance/Legal review regardless of stated reason).
- The refund amount exceeds **USD 500 / SGD 700** equivalent.
- The request is older than **90 days** from the original charge.
- The reason cited involves a **service outage, data loss, or security
  incident** — these are handled jointly with the Technical Escalations
  team and may involve service credits in addition to, or instead of, a
  refund.
- The customer's question references a product or refund scenario **not
  explicitly covered by Section 2 of this document.**

## 7. Refund Method & Timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method only, within 5–10
business days of approval; bank processing may add 3–5 additional business
days. Nimbus Cloud does not issue cash or store-credit refunds.

## 8. How Customers Request a Refund

1. Customer emails support or opens a ticket via the portal.
2. Agent identifies the specific product category (Section 2) — refund
   rules differ significantly between, e.g., Google Workspace and NetSuite.
3. If the request matches an eligible case in Sections 2, 3, or 5, and does
   not trigger any Section 6 condition, the agent may draft an approval
   citing the exact clause used.
4. If any Section 6 condition is met, or the product/scenario is not
   explicitly covered above, the agent must **not fabricate an answer** —
   the case is routed to a human Billing Specialist with the drafted
   context attached.
