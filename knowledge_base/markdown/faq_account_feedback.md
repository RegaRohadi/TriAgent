# PointStar FAQ — Account, Privacy & Feedback

**Document ID:** KB-FAQ-004
**Version:** 2.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-15
**Category:** Account / Feedback

---

**Q1. How do I update my company or contact information?**
Account Settings → Organization Profile. Changes to the primary billing
contact require confirmation from the current billing contact on file.

**Q2. How do I add or remove users (seats) on my account?**
Account Settings → Users & Roles. Adding users beyond your plan's included
seat count triggers a prorated charge for the remaining billing cycle.

**Q3. How can I submit product feedback or a feature request?**
Use the in-app Feedback widget (bottom-right on any page) or email
feedback@pointstar-consulting.example. All submissions are logged and reviewed monthly
by the Product team; we don't guarantee individual replies but do read every
submission.

**Q4. How is my data protected, and what is your data retention policy?**
As PointStar operates across Singapore, Malaysia, and Indonesia, we comply
with the relevant regime for each entity: Singapore PDPA, Malaysia PDPA, and
Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP). Data is retained for the
duration of an active subscription plus 30 days after cancellation, after
which it is permanently deleted unless a longer retention period is
contractually agreed (e.g. under a NetSuite or Professional Services SOW).

**Q5. How do I request deletion of my account and data?**
Submit a request via Account Settings → Privacy → Request Data Deletion, or
email privacy@pointstar-consulting.example. Deletion requests are processed within 30
days per PDPA requirements, after confirming identity of the requester.

**Q6. I had a bad support experience — how do I escalate a complaint?**
You can reply "escalate to supervisor" in any support thread, or email
feedback@pointstar-consulting.example with your ticket number. Complaints about service
quality are routed to a human Customer Success Manager, not resolved by the
automated system.

**Q7. Do you offer training or onboarding sessions?**
Yes, all Annual and Enterprise plans include a complimentary onboarding
session with a Solutions Consultant. Contact your Account Manager to
schedule.

**Q8. How do I know if my feedback resulted in a product change?**
Major feature releases referencing customer feedback are announced in our
release notes (accessible under Help → What's New) and via the feedback
widget when your specific submission is addressed.

**Q9. Can I change my subscription's primary administrator?**
Yes — the current admin can reassign the role under Users & Roles → Transfer
Admin Rights. If the current admin is unavailable, contact support with proof
of organizational authority to request a manual transfer.
